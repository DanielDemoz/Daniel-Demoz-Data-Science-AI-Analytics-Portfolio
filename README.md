# Daniel S. Demoz: Portfolio

Personal site for Daniel S. Demoz, a Toronto-based consultant, researcher, and educator focused on AI governance who also builds AI systems.

Live site: https://danieldemoz.github.io/Daniel-Demoz-Data-Science-AI-Analytics-Portfolio/

## What is on the site

- **About**: AI governance work, applied machine learning, research, and teaching.
- **Focus**: Govern, Build, and Teach.
- **Selected work**: the Public Funding Accountability Platform, three AI governance assessment tools, TTC Predict, and LoopLens.
- **Research and writing**: the book *Evidence-Based AI: The Discipline Behind Reliable AI*, two SSRN papers, and articles.
- **Experience**: Lead Consultant at BRUKD Consultancy and Part-Time Professor at Ontario colleges.
- **Education and credentials**: University of Ottawa MIA, Khon Kaen University, Centennial College, University of Asmara, and the IAPP AIGP (in progress).

## Stack

- HTML5 with Tailwind CSS (CDN)
- AOS for scroll animation
- Feather icons
- Hosted on GitHub Pages

## Preview locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server, for example `npx serve`.
